Reseller Commission Guide ​

For partners holding the RESELLER pricing tier. It covers what a reseller earns, when the money becomes withdrawable, and how a payout is requested.

A reseller pays silver prices. The RESELLER tier is priced identically to SILVER — same search prices, same booking limits. What the tier adds is a commission (cashback) per seated passenger, paid separately from the booking. Nothing in the price response distinguishes the two tiers; see Custom OTA → promoTierPrices for the one place that matters to a price consumer.

Overview ​

Who earnsThe organization, when the booking has one; an organization-less partner earns on their own user row.
Which bookingsCustom bookings only — the tier-priced flow. Regular OTA bookings earn nothing.
How muchA fixed SAR amount per seated passenger: one for adults, one for children. Infants earn nothing. Defaults are 5 SAR and 5 SAR; the live figures are set by an admin.
CurrencySAR is canonical. Every amount on these endpoints is SAR.
Bank feeAbsorbed by Ahlaan. The partner receives the full commission.

Which bookings count ​

A booking earns commission when the resolved effective pricing tier recorded on it at booking time was RESELLER. That is a frozen record, not a re-read of the account's tier today, which has two consequences worth knowing:

  • Bookings made before the tier was granted earn nothing. There is no backfill.
  • Being priced as a reseller is what counts. A caller whose promo access is switched off is priced as a holder, and a booking made that way carries no reseller tier and so earns nothing.

The two stages ​

A commission row is written in two steps, and only the second one is money you can ask for.

customer's money arrives  ->  ESTIMATED   (accrued, NOT payable)
booking closed (COMPLETED) ->  CONFIRMED  (payable)
claimed into a payout      ->  payoutId set, still CONFIRMED
transfer recorded          ->  paidAt set
StatusMeaning
ESTIMATEDThe invoice was paid. The figure is a projection from the passengers booked. It is shown to you, and it is not withdrawable.
CONFIRMEDThe booking was closed. This is the withdrawable figure.
VOIDThe booking was cancelled before the commission was paid. Nothing is owed.

Why the confirmed figure can be lower ​

The estimate prices every booked passenger. The confirmed figure prices the passengers whose tickets were actually delivered. When the ticket source under-delivers, the difference is deducted and both figures stay on the row:

FieldMeaning
estimatedPaxCount / estimatedAmountSARWhat the booking was expected to earn.
confirmedPaxCount / confirmedAmountSARWhat it actually earned.
unticketedPaxCountSeats that were booked and not ticketed.

Each un-ticketed seat is deducted at the adult amount. The ticketed count is a single total with no adult/child breakdown, so there is nothing to tell the system which of the missing seats was a child. While the two configured amounts are equal — as they are by default — the deduction is exact either way.

Tickets that arrive late can still be paid. If missing tickets are uploaded after the booking was closed, a second commission row is written automatically for the recovered seats, already CONFIRMED, priced at the same per-passenger amount as the original row.

⚠️ This does not apply once a shortfall refund has been settled. Settling the refund freezes the booking's ticketed count, so a later upload raising it is refused — you were refunded for those seats, and the tickets cannot arrive on top of the refund. On the current booking flow that is how a short delivery is always resolved, so in practice a closed short booking keeps the reduced figure.

Who may see the figures and request a payout ​

One account per organization — the account that registered the partnership, i.e. the user on the organization's mitra registration.

This is deliberately narrower than "any member". Requesting a payout is where the destination bank account is typed, so widening the read would also widen who can direct the organization's money.

SituationResult
You are the organization's registered accountFull access to these endpoints.
You are another member of that organization403
Your organization has no mitra registration403, naming the gap. An admin can still pay the partnership out.
You have no organization and hold the tier yourselfFull access to your own rows.

Endpoints ​

All require a customer bearer token.

EndpointMethodDescription
/api/v1/reseller-commissionsGETYour commission rows, newest first
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/summaryGETThe three totals plus the payout minimum
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payoutsGETYour payouts, newest first
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payoutsPOSTRequest a payout of everything available
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts/:id/cancelPOSTWithdraw a payout request

1. Commission totals ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/summary' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'

Real responses from one partner, at four points in the same run — before the invoice was paid, after it was paid, after the booking closed 2 seats short, and after the payout was transferred:

json
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "50", "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "40", "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "40", "minPayoutAmountSAR": "10" }

The second line is the accrual (8 adults + 2 children at 5 SAR each; the infant earns nothing). The third is the same money after the close, reduced by the 2 seats the ticket source did not deliver. The fourth is after the transfer — and minPayoutAmountSAR differs there because an admin lowered it between the third read and the fourth.

FieldMeaning
estimatedSARAccrued and not payable. The bookings behind it have not been closed, and the figure can come out lower.
availableSARConfirmed and not yet claimed into a payout. This is what a payout can draw on.
paidSARAlready transferred.
minPayoutAmountSARThe smallest payout the system will send.

0 is the absence of a floor, not a safe one. The value is returned even when it is 0 precisely so the screen shows what is in force rather than implying a rule that is not set. Display it.

Money in an open payout request is in none of the three. Once a request claims your confirmed rows they leave availableSAR, and they only reach paidSAR when the transfer is recorded. Between those two moments the amount is on the payout itself — read GET /payouts to show it.

2. List commission rows ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions?page=1&limit=20&status=CONFIRMED' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
QueryRequiredNotes
pagenoDefaults to 1
limitnoDefaults to 20, capped at 100 server-side
statusnoESTIMATED, CONFIRMED or VOID

The response is { "data": [...], "meta": { "total", "page", "limit", "totalPages" } }. Each row carries the frozen per-passenger amounts it was priced at (amountPerAdultSAR, amountPerChildSAR), both legs described above, the booking it was earned on (customBooking: code, departure date, route, status), payoutId once claimed, and paidAt once transferred.

Money fields are JSON strings, not numbers — and not padded to two decimals. A 40 SAR figure arrives as "40", and a 5 SAR rate as "5". They are exact decimals; parse them as decimals rather than as floats, and format them yourself for display rather than assuming the server already did.

A booking can have more than one row. sequence distinguishes them — 1 is the original accrual, and a later sequence is a top-up for tickets that arrived after the close.

3. Request a payout ​

bash
curl -X POST '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "destinationBankCode": "BCA",
    "destinationBankName": "Bank Central Asia",
    "destinationAccountNumber": "1234567890",
    "destinationAccountName": "PT Mitra Reseller"
  }'

This claims every confirmed, unpaid commission row you have into one payout, created as REQUESTED.

FieldRequired to requestRequired to be approved
destinationBankCodenono
destinationBankNamenono
destinationAccountNumbernoyes
destinationAccountNamenoyes

The destination is typed per payout — it is not a saved, verified account. Check it against your last payout (GET /payouts) before submitting: a typo sends the money to a stranger. An admin who did not request the payout has to approve the destination before anything is transferred, which is the reason a payout does not go straight to APPROVED. That approver can only accept or reject an account you typed — they cannot replace it — so what you send here is what the money goes to. Got it wrong? The payout is rejected and you request again with the right account.

⚠️ Always send the account number and name. The API accepts a request with them blank, and a blank one is the single case where the approver fills the destination in themselves — at which point nobody but that admin has seen where the money is going. Sending them is what keeps the second-person check meaningful.

StatusMeaning
400Nothing confirmed and unpaid is available, or the total is below minPayoutAmountSAR. The message names how much more must be confirmed first.
409Another payout claimed part of the same commission while your request was in flight. Nothing was paid out — re-read the balance and retry.

4. List your payouts ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts?page=1&limit=20' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
Payout statusMeaning
REQUESTEDWaiting for an admin to check the destination account. Still cancellable.
APPROVEDDestination checked. The transfer is being made.
PAIDTransferred. reference carries the bank reference and paidAt the timestamp.
REJECTEDRefused, with rejectionReason. The commission rows go back to available.
CANCELLEDWithdrawn by you. The commission rows go back to available.

totalAmountSAR is the canonical figure — what you were owed, in SAR. A payout sent in another currency also carries what actually left the bank (payoutCurrency, payoutAmount) and the rate it was converted at (payoutRateToSAR, SAR per one unit of that currency).

5. Cancel a payout request ​

bash
curl -X POST '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts/<payoutId>/cancel' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'

Releases the claimed commission back to available. Allowed only while the payout is REQUESTED — once a second person has approved the destination the transfer may already be in motion at the bank.

Somebody else's payout answers 404, not 403: confirming that the id exists would tell a stranger that money is waiting under it.

Becoming a reseller ​

The tier is chosen on the mitra registration form like SILVER and GOLD. Choosing it does not grant it: an admin confirms the refundable security deposit (10,000 SAR by default, and a promo can change what a given applicant is charged) and approves the registration first.
