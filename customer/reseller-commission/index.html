Reseller Commission Guide ​

For partners holding the RESELLER pricing tier. It covers what a reseller earns, when the money becomes withdrawable, and how a payout is requested.

A reseller pays silver prices. The RESELLER tier is priced identically to SILVER — same search prices, same booking limits. What the tier adds is a commission (cashback) per seated passenger, paid separately from the booking. Nothing in the price response distinguishes the two tiers; see Custom OTA → promoTierPrices for the one place that matters to a price consumer.

Overview ​

Who earnsThe organization, when the booking has one; an organization-less partner earns on their own user row.
Which bookingsCustom bookings only — the tier-priced flow. Regular OTA bookings earn nothing.
How muchA fixed SAR amount per seated passenger, per flight leg, at each leg's own cabin. Infants earn nothing. Defaults are 5 SAR in economy and 8 SAR in business, for adults and children alike; the live figures are set by an admin.
CurrencySAR is canonical. Every amount on these endpoints is SAR.
Bank feeAbsorbed by Ahlaan. The partner receives the full commission.

How the amount is worked out ​

Each flight leg earns on its own, at the rate for that leg's cabin. A booking has no single cabin: the class is recorded per leg, so a round trip can fly out in economy and back in business and each direction is priced for what it was.

  • A one-way booking earns one leg's worth.
  • A round trip earns two — so an all-economy round trip earns double what the same passengers earn one way.

Worked example — 8 adults and 2 children, economy out and business back, at the default 5 SAR and 8 SAR:

outbound (economy):  8 × 5 + 2 × 5  =  50 SAR
return   (business): 8 × 8 + 2 × 8  =  80 SAR
                             total  = 130 SAR

The rate and the cabin of both legs are frozen onto the commission row when it is written, so a later rate change never changes what an existing booking earned.

Which bookings count ​

A booking earns commission when the resolved effective pricing tier recorded on it at booking time was RESELLER. That is a frozen record, not a re-read of the account's tier today, which has two consequences worth knowing:

  • Bookings made before the tier was granted earn nothing. There is no backfill.
  • Being priced as a reseller is what counts. A caller whose promo access is switched off is priced as a holder, and a booking made that way carries no reseller tier and so earns nothing.

The two stages ​

A commission row is written in two steps, and only the second one is money you can ask for.

customer's money arrives  ->  ESTIMATED   (accrued, NOT payable)
booking closed (COMPLETED) ->  CONFIRMED  (payable)
claimed into a payout      ->  payoutId set, still CONFIRMED
transfer recorded          ->  paidAt set
StatusMeaning
ESTIMATEDThe invoice was paid. The figure is a projection from the passengers booked. It is shown to you, and it is not withdrawable.
CONFIRMEDThe booking was closed. This is the withdrawable figure.
VOIDThe booking was cancelled before the commission was paid. Nothing is owed.

Why the confirmed figure can be lower ​

The estimate prices every booked passenger. The confirmed figure prices the passengers whose tickets were actually delivered. When the ticket source under-delivers, the difference is deducted and both figures stay on the row:

FieldMeaning
estimatedPaxCount / estimatedAmountSARWhat the booking was expected to earn.
confirmedPaxCount / confirmedAmountSARWhat it actually earned.
unticketedPaxCountSeats that were booked and not ticketed, on the worse of the two legs.

⚠️ On a round trip, these counts and this amount answer different questions. The deduction is taken per leg — a passenger ticketed on the outbound leg but not the return one still earns the outbound commission — while the counts above stay seat counts and report the worse leg. So where the legs were short by different amounts, confirmedAmountSAR ÷ confirmedPaxCount is not a per-seat rate, and unticketedPaxCount is not the two legs added together. The commission is reconstructed from the per-leg rates, not from these counts.

Each un-ticketed seat is deducted at the adult amount. The ticketed count is a single total with no adult/child breakdown, so there is nothing to tell the system which of the missing seats was a child. While the adult and child amounts of a cabin are equal — as they are by default — the deduction is exact either way.

On a round trip the deduction covers both legs. A passenger who was never ticketed flew neither direction, so a missing seat costs the two legs' adult amounts added together. On the worked example above that is 5 + 8 = 13 SAR per missing seat.

Tickets that arrive late can still be paid. If missing tickets are uploaded after the booking was closed, a second commission row is written automatically for the recovered seats, already CONFIRMED, priced at the same per-passenger amounts across the same legs as the original row — so it gives back exactly what the shortfall took off, even if an admin changed the rates in between.

⚠️ This does not apply once a shortfall refund has been settled. Settling the refund freezes the booking's ticketed count, so a later upload raising it is refused — you were refunded for those seats, and the tickets cannot arrive on top of the refund. On the current booking flow that is how a short delivery is always resolved, so in practice a closed short booking keeps the reduced figure.

Who may see the figures ​

One account per organization — the account that registered the partnership, i.e. the user on the organization's mitra registration.

This is deliberately narrower than "any member": an organization's earnings and the accounts they were paid to are not something every member should read.

⛔ These endpoints are read-only. You cannot request a payout and cannot withdraw one. Ahlaan's finance team makes the transfer and then records it, so a payout appears in your list once the money has already been sent.

SituationResult
You are the organization's registered accountFull access to these endpoints.
You are another member of that organization403
Your organization has no mitra registration403, naming the gap. An admin can still pay the partnership out.
You have no organization and hold the tier yourselfFull access to your own rows.

Endpoints ​

All require a customer bearer token.

EndpointMethodDescription
/api/v1/reseller-commissionsGETYour commission rows, newest first
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/summaryGETThe three totals plus the payout minimum
/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payoutsGETYour payouts, newest first

⚠️ Removed. POST /api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts (request a payout) and POST /api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts/:id/cancel (withdraw one) no longer exist and answer 404. Payouts are recorded by Ahlaan's finance team after the transfer has been made; there is nothing to request and nothing to withdraw.

1. Commission totals ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/summary' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'

Real responses from one partner, at four points in the same run — before the invoice was paid, after it was paid, after the booking closed 2 seats short, and after the payout was transferred:

json
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "50", "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "40", "paidSAR": "0",  "minPayoutAmountSAR": "100" }
{ "estimatedSAR": "0",  "availableSAR": "0",  "paidSAR": "40", "minPayoutAmountSAR": "10" }

The second line is the accrual (8 adults + 2 children at 5 SAR each; the infant earns nothing). The third is the same money after the close, reduced by the 2 seats the ticket source did not deliver. The fourth is after the transfer — and minPayoutAmountSAR differs there because an admin lowered it between the third read and the fourth.

FieldMeaning
estimatedSARAccrued and not payable. The bookings behind it have not been closed, and the figure can come out lower.
availableSARConfirmed and not yet claimed into a payout. This is what a payout can draw on.
paidSARAlready transferred.
minPayoutAmountSARThe smallest payout the system will send.

0 is the absence of a floor, not a safe one. The value is returned even when it is 0 precisely so the screen shows what is in force rather than implying a rule that is not set. Display it.

Money in an open payout request is in none of the three. Once a request claims your confirmed rows they leave availableSAR, and they only reach paidSAR when the transfer is recorded. Between those two moments the amount is on the payout itself — read GET /payouts to show it.

2. List commission rows ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions?page=1&limit=20&status=CONFIRMED' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
QueryRequiredNotes
pagenoDefaults to 1
limitnoDefaults to 20, capped at 100 server-side
statusnoESTIMATED, CONFIRMED or VOID

The response is { "data": [...], "meta": { "total", "page", "limit", "totalPages" } }. Each row carries the frozen per-passenger amounts it was priced at, both stages described above, the booking it was earned on, payoutId once claimed, and paidAt once transferred.

displayStatus — the label to show ​

⛔ PAID is not a stored status. status stays ESTIMATED | CONFIRMED | VOID; a paid row still reads CONFIRMED and carries payoutId and paidAt. So the API sends a ready-made label beside it rather than changing what status means:

displayStatusWhat it means for you
ESTIMATEDThe booking is not closed yet. Not payable
AVAILABLEPayable — this is what availableSAR counts
IN_PAYOUTAlready claimed into a payout you requested. The money is committed and cannot be claimed twice
PAIDTransferred
VOIDThe booking was cancelled

payout sits beside it — null when the row is unclaimed, otherwise { "id", "status" } so a row can be traced to the payout holding it.

IN_PAYOUT is why your list can show more rows than your available balance covers. A payout is recorded and paid in one step now, so a row normally goes straight from AVAILABLE to PAID. IN_PAYOUT still describes a row claimed by an older payout that never completed: CONFIRMED, no paidAt, and correctly excluded from availableSAR. Reading status alone makes it look payable twice.

The booking block ​

jsonc
"customBooking": {
  "id": "...",
  "bookingCode": "...",
  "status": "TICKET_UPLOADED_PARTIAL",
  "tripType": "ROUND_TRIP",
  "origin": "JED", "originName": "...",
  "destination": "MED", "destinationName": "...",
  "departureDate": "...", "returnDate": null,
  "createdAt": "...",                // when you made the booking
  "paxCounts": { "adult": 2, "child": 1, "infant": 0 },
  "contact": { "email": "...", "phone": "..." }
}

contact is the contact person you entered when you created the booking. It is null if that record cannot be read as an email-and-phone pair. Passenger names are not on this list — open the booking for those.

The amounts are recorded per flight leg:

FieldMeaning
amountPerAdultSAR / amountPerChildSARThe outbound leg's rates
returnAmountPerAdultSAR / returnAmountPerChildSARThe return leg's rates. Empty on a one-way booking
outboundTripClass / returnTripClassThe cabin each leg was priced at: ECONOMY, BUSINESS or UNKNOWN

An empty value is information, not a gap. Empty return fields mean the booking has no return leg. An empty outboundTripClass means the row was written before per-leg pricing existed, and its two amountPer*SAR fields are the single rate that priced the whole booking. UNKNOWN means the leg exists but its stored cabin could not be read, so it was priced at the economy rate.

Two real rows from the same partner, captured 2026-08-13 at economy 5 SAR and business 8 SAR, both on 8 adults + 2 children + 1 infant. The money fields are trimmed to the ones this section is about.

A round trip, economy out and business back, closed 2 seats short:

json
{
  "status": "CONFIRMED",
  "amountPerAdultSAR": "5",
  "amountPerChildSAR": "5",
  "returnAmountPerAdultSAR": "8",
  "returnAmountPerChildSAR": "8",
  "outboundTripClass": "ECONOMY",
  "returnTripClass": "BUSINESS",
  "estimatedPaxCount": 10,
  "estimatedAmountSAR": "130",
  "confirmedPaxCount": 8,
  "unticketedPaxCount": 2,
  "confirmedAmountSAR": "104",
  "customBooking": { "bookingCode": "4HS3WEG", "status": "COMPLETED" }
}

130 is the two legs added together — 8 × 5 + 2 × 5 = 50 out, and 8 × 8 + 2 × 8 = 80 back. 104 is what survived the 2 missing seats: each one cost 5 + 8 = 13, because a passenger who was never ticketed flew neither direction.

A one-way economy booking, still estimated. The return half is empty:

json
{
  "status": "ESTIMATED",
  "amountPerAdultSAR": "5",
  "amountPerChildSAR": "5",
  "returnAmountPerAdultSAR": null,
  "returnAmountPerChildSAR": null,
  "outboundTripClass": "ECONOMY",
  "returnTripClass": null,
  "estimatedPaxCount": 10,
  "estimatedAmountSAR": "50",
  "customBooking": { "bookingCode": "R7CD9GK", "status": "WAITING_FOR_TICKET" }
}

⚠️ estimatedPaxCount is 10 on both, and that is not a mistake on the round trip. It counts SEATS, never legs, so it does not double when the money does — and the infant is excluded from it, which is why it is 10 rather than 11. Dividing an amount by a passenger count therefore does not give you a rate on a round trip; read the rate columns instead.

Money fields are JSON strings, not numbers — and not padded to two decimals. A 40 SAR figure arrives as "40", and a 5 SAR rate as "5". They are exact decimals; parse them as decimals rather than as floats, and format them yourself for display rather than assuming the server already did.

A booking can have more than one row. sequence distinguishes them — 1 is the original accrual, and a later sequence is a top-up for tickets that arrived after the close.

3. How you get paid ​

You do not request a payout. Ahlaan's finance team makes the transfer and then records it, so a payout appears in your list once the money has already been sent.

⚠️ Behaviour change. POST /api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts used to claim every confirmed, unpaid row into a REQUESTED payout awaiting an admin's approval. It is gone and now answers 404, along with the cancel route. Nothing replaces them on this API.

Give finance your bank details out of band — the account is typed by the admin recording the payout, not read from your profile, and nothing on this API stores one for you. GET /payouts shows the destination each past payout went to, which is the record to check if a transfer lands somewhere unexpected.

Watch availableSAR on the summary to know what is payable. minPayoutAmountSAR is the floor a payout has to clear; below it, finance will wait for more commission to confirm rather than send a transfer that costs more to make than it is worth.

4. List your payouts ​

bash
curl -X GET '/api/v1/reseller-commissions/payouts?page=1&limit=20' \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer <token>'
Payout statusMeaning
PAIDTransferred. reference carries the bank reference and paidAt the timestamp. The only status a payout is written with today.
REQUESTEDLegacy. Was waiting for an admin to check the destination account.
APPROVEDLegacy. Destination checked, transfer being made.
REJECTEDLegacy. Refused, with rejectionReason. The commission rows went back to available.
CANCELLEDLegacy. Withdrawn by you. The commission rows went back to available.

The four legacy statuses come from the request-and-approve flow that no longer exists. No payout can reach them again, and none of the ones that carry them can change.

totalAmountSAR is the canonical figure — what you were owed, in SAR. A paid payout also records what left the bank: payoutCurrency, payoutAmount and the rate it was converted at, payoutRate. A SAR payout is the identity case, so those read back as the SAR total at a rate of 1.

payoutRate is not SAR per unit — read it through payoutRateDirection on the same payout. SAR_TO_FOREIGN means divide the amount by the rate, FOREIGN_TO_SAR means multiply. A new IDR payout is SAR_TO_FOREIGN, so its rate is IDR per one SAR (~4250) and payoutAmount ÷ payoutRate is the SAR figure. Direction is stored per row: a payout recorded before this convention shipped carries FOREIGN_TO_SAR and still reads correctly through it.

5. Reading a payout ​

Each entry in GET /payouts carries status, totalAmountSAR, paidAt, the bank reference, and the destination account it was sent to.

status reads PAID on every payout recorded now. Older entries may read REQUESTED, APPROVED, REJECTED or CANCELLED — those come from the request-and-approve flow that no longer exists, and none of them can change again. A REJECTED or CANCELLED payout released its commission rows back to available at the time, so that money is either payable again or already inside a later payout.

The destination fields can be null. They are optional on the record, so a payout made to an account finance already held may name only the reference.

Becoming a reseller ​

The tier is chosen on the mitra registration form like SILVER and GOLD. Choosing it does not grant it: an admin confirms the refundable security deposit (10,000 SAR by default, and a promo can change what a given applicant is charged) and approves the registration first.
