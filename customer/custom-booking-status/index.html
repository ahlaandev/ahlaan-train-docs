Custom Booking Status & Confirmation ​

This guide covers the customer-facing view of a custom booking's lifecycle — what each status means, when you receive your ticket, and how to confirm receipt to close out the booking.

Quick Overview ​

ActionEndpointAuth
Confirm ticket received (legacy — see note below)PATCH /api/v1/custom-booking/:id/confirm-receivedAuthenticated or guest (email + phone)

What changed. Under the current flow, an admin uploads your ticket and records how many passengers it covers — that upload sends nothing and your ticket stays hidden from you while it awaits review. A second admin then reviews it; approving it is what opens your download and sends the ticket email. Our team closes the booking afterwards, and that step is internal and sends nothing. You no longer need to confirm receipt. The PATCH .../confirm-received endpoint is kept only for bookings that were already in TICKET_RECEIVED under the previous flow.

Internal margin data is not exposed. Customer-facing responses (GET /custom-booking/:id, GET /custom-booking, and the create/update responses) never include capitalPriceTotalSAR or the entire pricingSnapshot object. pricingSnapshot (supplier fare, reducer, capital price, special-B2B FX levers, capital-price history) is returned only on the admin custom-booking endpoints. Do not build customer UI against it — use the top-level payable fields (pricePerAdultSAR, totalPriceSAR / IDR / USD) and isSpecialB2B instead.

Lifecycle from Your Perspective ​

StatusWhat it means for you
PENDINGWe received your booking. Please submit your passenger list to move forward.
UPLOADED_MANIFESTYour passenger list has been submitted. Normally a brief, transient state — your booking is confirmed automatically and moves to CONFIRMED. It only lingers here if the operator has turned off auto-confirm, in which case our team confirms it manually.
CONFIRMEDYour booking is confirmed and your invoice is issued. We email you a confirmation. To pay, choose a payment method via POST /invoice/:id/generate-payment — we then send you the matching instructions (Xendit link, or SAR/USD bank-transfer details) by WhatsApp (or email). You may cancel at this stage via PATCH /custom-booking/:id/status.
WAITING_FOR_PAYMENTYour invoice is ready — please complete payment using the instructions we sent after you picked a payment method.
WAITING_PAYMENT_REVIEWManual transfer only. We've received your transfer proof and our team is verifying it. If approved, the booking moves to PAID. If we need a corrected proof, the booking returns to WAITING_FOR_PAYMENT so you can re-upload.
PAIDLegacy only. Older bookings briefly showed this after payment. New bookings move straight to WAITING_FOR_TICKET once payment is received.
WAITING_FOR_TICKETPayment received — the channel is preparing your ticket. New bookings reach this state automatically as soon as your payment settles. This is also what you see if a ticket has already arrived but our team hasn't finished checking it yet — see the next two rows.
TICKET_UPLOADED_FULLYour tickets have arrived — every travelling passenger who needs one has a ticket on file. They are not available yet: a second admin has to review the upload first, and until that happens you keep seeing WAITING_FOR_TICKET instead of this status, with nothing to download. Once reviewed and released, the status updates, the file becomes downloadable, and we email it to you (see Ticket Email). A later, corrected upload on a booking that has already been released downloads immediately with no new email.
TICKET_UPLOADED_PARTIALThe airline could only issue tickets for some of your passengers. Same review step as above: until a second admin releases the upload you see WAITING_FOR_TICKET, not this status, and there is nothing to download; once released, the tickets that did arrive become downloadable and we email them to you (see Ticket Email). The passengers we could not ticket are owed their money back, so the booking stays open while our team arranges that refund. The email tells you your ticket is ready; it does not say how many passengers it covers. Check the file, and our team contacts you directly about the refund.
SHORTFALL_REFUNDEDThe money for the passengers we could not ticket has been sent back to you. Unlike the two rows above, you see this status as-is even before a second admin has released your ticket — but the download and the email still wait on that release, so SHORTFALL_REFUNDED alone does not mean the file is ready yet. The booking stays open until our team closes it, and no further email is sent automatically once you have been emailed. Once the refund is recorded the shortfall is settled, and the tickets we could not issue are not added to this booking afterwards — you have already been paid for them. Contact our team if you still need those seats.
TICKET_RECEIVEDLegacy only. Older bookings that are still waiting for your receipt confirmation. New bookings never enter this state.
COMPLETEDBooking finalized — our team has verified your tickets and closed the booking. No email is sent at this point; you were emailed your tickets when they were released.
CANCELLEDBooking cancelled.
EXPIREDPayment window elapsed before payment was received.
NOT_AVAILABLEThe channel could not provide seats for this booking.

Payment Instructions ​

When your booking reaches CONFIRMED (normally right after you submit your passenger list — see auto-confirm), we email you a confirmation. We do not send payment details yet — first you choose how you want to pay.

Call POST /invoice/:id/generate-payment with your chosen method (see payment options — pay in IDR via Xendit, or in SAR/USD via manual bank transfer). As soon as you do, we deliver the matching instructions to you:

  • Xendit — a hosted payment link.
  • SAR / USD manual transfer — the destination bank account (bank name, account number, account holder) to transfer to, plus the exact amount.

Either way, delivery prefers WhatsApp (to the contact phone on your booking) and falls back to email if there is no phone or the WhatsApp message can't be sent. The message includes your booking code, route, departure date, passenger count and the amount due.

Delivery is best-effort — if you don't receive the instructions within a few minutes, contact support with your booking code (re-sending the same method shortly after is de-duplicated, so it won't arrive twice; choosing a different method does send fresh instructions). You can also always retrieve the invoice/payment state via GET /custom-booking/:id, the available methods via GET /invoice/:id/payment-options, and — for a Xendit payment — the link itself on the generate-payment response.

Ticket Email ​

You receive this email once your ticket is released by a second admin — not when it is first uploaded, which sends nothing and leaves your ticket hidden. Release can happen from any status your ticket is delivered from — TICKET_UPLOADED_FULL, TICKET_UPLOADED_PARTIAL, SHORTFALL_REFUNDED, and also TICKET_RECEIVED or COMPLETED on an older booking that had its ticket uploaded after it was closed. Until release you keep seeing WAITING_FOR_TICKET on the first two (SHORTFALL_REFUNDED shows as itself either way, and a closed booking shows as closed). You do not wait for the booking to be closed — only for that release. The subject is:

Konfirmasi Tiket Kereta Anda - {bookingCode}

The email includes:

  • Booking code, route, departure date, total price.
  • The passenger count, on a full delivery only — see below.
  • The ticket file as a downloadable attachment.

On a partial delivery the email does not state a passenger count and does not say your passenger data is complete, because at that point it is not. It tells you the ticket is issued and can be downloaded. It also does not mention the refund you are owed — our team contacts you about that separately.

You are emailed once. If the remaining tickets arrive later, or a corrected file is uploaded, no new email is sent automatically — the booking page always has the current file, and support can trigger a manual resend on request.

The email is delivered asynchronously via our third-party provider and is not automatically retried by the queue worker — if you don't see it within a few minutes, check spam first, then contact support with your booking code and they can trigger a manual resend. See Tickets & Email for general email/ ticket-download guidance.

Confirm Ticket Received (legacy) ​

Most customers can skip this section. It only applies to bookings that were left in TICKET_RECEIVED under the previous flow. New bookings are closed by our team after the uploaded ticket is verified.

Once you've received and verified your ticket, confirm receipt to move the booking to COMPLETED. The booking must currently be in TICKET_RECEIVED.

http
PATCH /custom-booking/:id/confirm-received

Authenticated user ​

bash
curl -X PATCH /api/v1/custom-booking/booking-123/confirm-received \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <jwt-token>"

Guest user ​

Guest access requires both email and phone query parameters matching the booking's contactPerson.

bash
curl -X PATCH '/api/v1/custom-booking/booking-123/confirm-received?email=guest@example.com&phone=628123456789'

Response (200) ​

json
{
  "id": "booking-123",
  "bookingCode": "ABC1234",
  "status": "COMPLETED",
  "ticketChannel": { "id": "0190abc1-...", "name": "HHR" }
}

Errors ​

  • 400 Bad Request — Booking is not in TICKET_RECEIVED.
  • 401 Unauthorized — Guest verification (email + phone) is required and missing.
  • 403 Forbidden — Authenticated user is not the booking owner.
  • 404 Not Found — Booking not found, or guest email/phone do not match.

Note for admin callers. This route stays reachable with a real admin token — there is no admin equivalent to redirect to — but the response comes back with every money field removed: the six selling prices, capitalPriceTotalSAR, pricingSnapshot, and the whole invoice block. invoiceId and exchangeRateId still return. Booking money for an admin lives on GET /api/v1/admin/custom-booking/:id, which splits it across two permission codes — custom-bookings:read-pricing for the selling prices and custom-bookings:read-capital-markup for the capital price and the markup. Customers, guests, and admins using an "act as customer" impersonation token are unaffected.

PATCH /custom-booking/:id/status (cancel) behaves differently — it answers 403 to a real admin token and names PATCH /api/v1/admin/custom-booking/:id/status as the replacement. See Custom Booking.

Auto-Completion (legacy) ​

For bookings still in TICKET_RECEIVED under the legacy flow: if you don't confirm within 24 hours after the departure date, the system automatically transitions the booking TICKET_RECEIVED → COMPLETED. The booking response will include an autoCompletedAt timestamp in that case, instead of being null.

New bookings never reach TICKET_RECEIVED at all, so autoCompletedAt stays null and this cron does not apply to them. Uploading the ticket records how many passengers were ticketed and parks the booking at TICKET_UPLOADED_FULL or TICKET_UPLOADED_PARTIAL; our team closes it from there, and nothing transitions into TICKET_RECEIVED any more. The cron therefore only ever acts on bookings that predate that change.

You can still view the booking after auto-completion; no further customer action is required.
