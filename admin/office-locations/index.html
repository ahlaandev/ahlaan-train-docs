Office Locations - Admin Management ​

Admin-only guide for managing the physical Ahlaan offices used by the IN_PERSON payment rail: the offices a customer can walk into and settle a custom-booking invoice in cash.

All endpoints require:

  • Authorization: Bearer <jwt> with a user that has the ADMIN role.
  • An Access Role granting office-locations:manage. Every route (list, detail, create, update, toggle-active, delete) needs it. See Access Roles.
  • Base path: /api/v1/admin/office-locations

Why this exists ​

The IN_PERSON rail needs a destination the customer can be sent to, exactly the way the manual bank transfer rail needs a destination bank account. This module is the office equivalent of Manual Transfer bank accounts.

RailDestination registrySelected by
Manual bank transferManualTransferBankAccountCustomer picks, or auto-picked by priority
In-person officeOfficeLocationAlways auto-picked by priority. There is no customer picker.

Selection is server-side only. The customer never chooses an office and the API accepts no office id from the client. The IN_PERSON rail always picks the highest-priority active office for the currency, ordered priority descending then createdAt ascending. priority is therefore the operational lever that decides where customers are sent.

On a fresh environment with no offices configured, this is simply [] - and that is enough to disable the IN_PERSON rail entirely, even with the feature flag on. See No office means no rail.

  • Both query parameters are optional.
  • The admin list includes soft-deleted rows so history stays visible. Filter them out client-side on deletedAt.
  • Ordered priority descending, then createdAt ascending. This is the same order the IN_PERSON auto-pick uses, so the first active SAR row in this list is the office customers will be sent to.

Get one ​

bash
GET /api/v1/admin/office-locations/:id

Same single-object shape as Create. 404 when the office does not exist.

Update ​

bash
PATCH /api/v1/admin/office-locations/:id

Partial update: only the fields you send are written. code and currency are uppercased.

  • 404 when the office does not exist.
  • 409 when the new code collides with another office.

Toggle active ​

bash
PATCH /api/v1/admin/office-locations/:id/toggle-active

Flips isActive. Deactivating removes the office from the auto-pick immediately. Payments already issued against it keep pointing at it, so a customer already on their way to that office still sees the correct address.

Delete (soft) ​

bash
DELETE /api/v1/admin/office-locations/:id
  • Always a soft delete. Sets isActive=false and stamps deletedAt. There is no hard delete on this resource.
  • Rationale: a settled payment freezes the office it was issued against. Hard-deleting the row would orphan that payment history.
  • 409 when the office is already deleted.

This differs deliberately from the manual-transfer bank accounts endpoint, which hard-deletes an account that no proof references. Offices are never hard-deleted regardless of reference count.

No office means no rail ​

OFFICE_SETTLEMENT_ENABLED=true is not sufficient on its own. The IN_PERSON rail auto-picks the highest-priority active office for the settlement currency, so with no active SAR office there is nothing to pick and the rail is silently unavailable.

Verified behaviour with the flag on and the only SAR office deactivated:

SurfaceWith an active officeWith none
payment-optionsinPerson.enabled: true, office populatedinPerson.enabled: false, office: null, amount: 0
available-gatewaysIN_PERSON presentIN_PERSON absent from the list

Degradation is graceful - nothing errors - which also means a deploy can look healthy while the rail is entirely dark. Seed at least one active SAR office in every environment where the flag is on, and treat office creation as part of enabling the feature rather than a later step.

Switching offices ​

To move customers from one office to another:

  1. Create the new office with a higher priority than the current one.
  2. Deactivate the old office (toggle-active) once you are ready.

Do not delete the old office. Payments already issued against it must stay readable.

Rate limits ​

All write routes (POST, PATCH, DELETE) are throttled to 20 requests per 60 seconds. Reads are not separately throttled beyond the global default.
